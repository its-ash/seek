What Seek does

Pick the lost device, then walk around. The meter, arrow and beeps tell you when you get closer.

Device list

Paired devices first, with names and type icons, then nearby ones found by a Bluetooth LE scan and classic discovery. Each shows its last signal (dBm) and "last seen". Nameless noise can be hidden.

Hot / cold finder

A big meter from the smoothed signal strength, a closer or farther arrow, and a rough distance clearly marked "approx.".

Beep and vibration

Both speed up as you get closer. Each can be switched off.

Signal lost

If the device has not been heard for 8 seconds, Seek says the signal is lost instead of guessing.

Play sound

For connected paired audio devices, a button plays a loud tone through them so you can hear a lost earbud. It stops by itself after 20 seconds.

Tips built in

Earbuds often only advertise when they are out of the case, so Seek reminds you to open the lid.

How to use it

Four steps, no setup.

  1. Allow Bluetooth scanningAllow the Nearby devices permission and keep Bluetooth on.
  2. Pick your deviceChoose it from the list of paired and nearby devices. For earbuds, open the case lid.
  3. Walk and watch the meterFollow the closer / farther arrow and the beeps. The faster they go, the nearer you are.
  4. Make it ringFor connected earbuds, tap Play sound to hear a loud tone from them.

Permissions and privacy

No account, no location tracking, no analytics. Scans stop when you leave the screen or the app. The only call to the internet is the update check in Settings.

What it asks for, and why

  • Nearby devices (Android 12+): to scan for and read paired Bluetooth devices.
  • Location (Android 11 and older): Android requires it for any Bluetooth scan. Seek does not use or store it.
  • Vibrate: for the vibration feedback.
  • Internet: the update check only.

Honest limits

  • Distance is approximate. Walls, bodies and pockets change the signal, so trust the meter and arrow over the number.
  • The device must be powered on and sending a Bluetooth signal.
  • Earbuds in their case usually stay silent until the lid is open.
  • Play sound works only for connected, paired audio devices.

Requirements and install

Requirements

  • Bluetooth LE on your phone.
